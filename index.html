2D Impedance Plane Bullseye

Present the load RL to the source as exactly 50 + j0 Ω at ω₀. The horizontal axis is resistance R and the vertical axis is reactance X, both in ohms. Step 1: the shunt C pulls Z₂ = RL ∥ 1/(jω₀C) round the dashed capacitive semicircle until its real part lands on the gold R = 50 Ω line. Step 2: the series L adds +jω₀L, lifting Z₂ straight up into the bullseye.

Interactive Smith Chart L-Match Playground

Match a resistive load RL to Z₀ = 50 Ω at the carrier ω₀. A shunt C across the load slides z along the load's dashed constant-conductance circle. A series L then slides it along a constant-resistance circle. Use C to land z₂ on the gold r = 1 circle, then use L to cancel its reactance and hit the bullseye.

Unity-Gain Op-Amp Buffer — From Ideal Follower to Ringing & Oscillation

V+ = Vin and V− = Vout, so Vout = A(s)(V+ − V−) gives HCL = A / (1 + A). Where |A| = 1, the denominator is 1 + 1∠φu.

  1. Inspect the unity-gain feedback loop
  2. Test Phase = −180° at Gain = 1 (Division by Zero!)
  3. Compare the Uncompensated Amplifier vs. −135° Target
  4. Observe the closed-loop Step Response ringing & oscillation
